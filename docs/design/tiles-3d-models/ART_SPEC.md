# V2 tile art specification

- Names: **Timber, Stone, Fleece, Grain, Clay, Desert**. Use `terrain_V2_flat/mid/max.png`, lower-case terrain keys in scripts, descriptive own-model names; no borrowed game names.
- Number plate: nominal hex side **1**, centre **(-0.36, -0.30)** in the game's screen-down lattice coordinates, or **(-0.36, +0.30)** in Blender's screen-up ground plane. Radius **0.30**. `MainActivity.kt`, `drawTile` → `drawNumberToken`, draws an upright circle at every tilt. Its centre's vertical offset scales with `cos(tilt)`; its radius does not.
- Every terrain reserves a **flat, empty ground circle of radius 0.345** (15% larger). Forest glade / rock shelf / trampled pasture / threshed wheat / levelled clay yard / sand pan. Blend the edge into local terrain; keep props, canopy overhangs and moving layers outside it. Desert reserves the space even though its game tile is normally unnumbered.
- Camera faces from **world -Y**. Keep foreground ground and props low enough to miss the upright plate at **55°**, including a safety apron beyond the projected clearing ellipse. Put tall trees, peaks and buildings behind or beside it. Also protect the next rear neighbours? plates; the shared height guard uses their nominal hex centres (??3/2, +1.5). Validate with real-size round plates at **0°, 27.5°, 55°**, and depth-sort tile-plus-plate layers in a mixed neighbour proof. Never hide collisions by painting every plate on top of the whole board.
- Hex: pointy-top, visible inset radius **0.93**, width **1.6108**, base top **0**, bottom **-0.22**, bevel **0.018**; retain the existing V2 edge material. Terrain clearing surface sits just above the base to avoid coincident-face artifacts.
- Orthographic transparent RGBA, **512 × 512**, camera scale **2.65**; upper-left sun plus soft sky. Palette: moss greens, warm dark rock and ivory snow, golden wheat, rust clay, ochre sand; wood **#4A3020** and old brass for review sheets.
- Assets must be **our own or clearly CC0**, recorded in `CREDITS.md`. These models, effects and proof plates are own procedural assets. Keep TileLife as the overlay layer; apply the same plate exclusion mask after displacement, interpolation and blur.

Reproduce sequentially with `tools/blender/run_token_clearings.ps1`. No application changes are part of this art pass.

- **Fleece means alpaca fleece:** 6?8 alpacas in small groups, long fluffy necks and upright ears, cream/white/fawn/brown coats; grazing and one or two looking up. Keep the lush pasture, wall/gate and visibly smaller horned, bearded goats. Motion: gentle shifts, grazing neck bends and head turns, outside the number clearing.
- **Clay colour:** a restrained terracotta/brick-red ground, terraces and cut faces; wet bottom darker red-brown. Keep the kiln and brick stacks distinct, and Clay clearly redder than Desert ochre.
